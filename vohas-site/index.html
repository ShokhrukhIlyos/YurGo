Our Story

Named after the lake, built for the moment.

Vohas takes its name from Lake Vohas in Termez. Inside, the concept is anything but traditional: one of the most eclectic menus in the city, moving easily from a full English breakfast to Uzbek mastava, from wood-fired pizza to sushi rolls, from mini steak sandwiches to ice baklava.

The dining room is modern and clean, the music comfortable, the atmosphere easy for families, couples, groups and vegetarians alike. Come for a slow breakfast, an afternoon coffee, or a full dinner with the table full.
