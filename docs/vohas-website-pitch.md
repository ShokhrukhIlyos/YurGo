## What You Get

### 🍽️ A Menu That Sells Itself
Every dish, with a photo, a description, and a price — organized into clean categories (Breakfast, Coffee & Drinks, Salads & Soups, Mains & Steaks, Pizza/Burgers/Kebabs, Sushi & Rolls). No more squinting at a laminated paper menu under low restaurant lighting.

### 📱 Scan-and-See Table Menus
50 unique QR codes, one per table. A customer sits down, scans the code on their own phone, and instantly sees your full menu — no app to download, no waiting for a waiter to bring a menu. It works even when the restaurant is busy and every server is slammed.

### 🌍 Speaks Your Customer's Language
Full support for **English, Russian, and Uzbek** — switch with one tap. Tourists, regional visitors, and locals all get a menu they can actually read.

### 📞 One Tap to Reach You
Call button, Instagram link, and Google Maps directions are all built into the site. A customer deciding between three restaurants picks the one that's easiest to act on — yours.

### 🖥️ Looks Great Everywhere
Built mobile-first because that's how 90%+ of people will find you — on their phone, standing outside, deciding where to eat. It also looks sharp on a desktop for anyone planning ahead.

### ⚡ Fast, Reliable, Always On
Hosted on modern cloud infrastructure (Netlify) with a global content delivery network. The site loads in a blink, whether the customer is next door or across the world researching their trip.
