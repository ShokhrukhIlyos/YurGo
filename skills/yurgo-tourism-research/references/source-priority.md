# Source Priority For YurGo Tourism Research

Use this reference when explaining how data, feedback, images, and locations were collected.

## Most Useful Sources So Far

### 1. Uzbekistan Travel

Most useful for:

- Official destination lists
- Short history and cultural significance
- Regional tourism positioning
- Nature, historical, pilgrimage, and museum descriptions
- Initial must-see lists

Limitations:

- Coordinates may be embedded in maps or missing.
- Opening hours, prices, access rules, and road conditions are often incomplete.
- Image reuse permission is not automatically clear.

### 2. Yandex Maps

Most useful for:

- Local place listings
- Ratings and review counts
- Local visitor feedback
- Photos and current public map presence
- Addresses and business status

Limitations:

- Reviews need translation and theme extraction.
- Some similarly named places may be unrelated.
- Photos are not automatically reusable.

### 3. Google Maps

Most useful for:

- User-provided pins
- Public map links
- Place existence and user photos
- Later manual verification by local team

Limitations:

- Short links may not expose coordinates through automated fetch.
- Reviews/photos are not automatically reusable.

### 4. Tripadvisor

Most useful for:

- Foreign-tourist feedback
- Ratings and review counts
- Practical travel tips, especially guides, transport, and route difficulty
- Understanding what visitors find memorable or confusing

Limitations:

- Some places have few reviews.
- User reviews are subjective.
- Do not copy long review text into app content.

### 5. Mapcarta / OpenStreetMap

Most useful for:

- Coordinates
- OSM object IDs
- Alternative names
- Nearby places
- Basic classification

Limitations:

- Open map data can contain errors.
- Needs confirmation for visitor entrances, parking, and access.

### 6. Wikidata / Wikimedia Commons

Most useful for:

- Coordinates for heritage objects
- Alternative spellings
- Wikimedia image categories
- Potentially reusable images with attribution

Limitations:

- Check each image license individually.
- Coordinates may describe the monument, not the visitor entrance.

### 7. UNESCO / Heritage Sources

Most useful for:

- Cultural significance
- Tentative-list heritage context
- Area-level coordinates
- Historical importance

Limitations:

- Coordinates can be approximate area centers.
- Not always practical for modern visitors.

### 8. Academic / Museum Sources

Most useful for:

- Deep historical accuracy
- Archaeology and chronology
- Important discoveries and scholarly interpretation

Limitations:

- Too technical for app copy.
- Usually weak on visitor logistics.

### 9. Local News / Government Updates

Most useful for:

- New tourism projects
- Infrastructure development
- New complexes such as Termez Ethno-Town
- Policy and investment updates

Limitations:

- May describe planned projects before full public visitor details exist.

### 10. Travel Agencies And Blogs

Most useful for:

- Route ideas
- Day-trip structure
- Practical descriptions of remote places

Limitations:

- Commercial bias is possible.
- Verify facts against stronger sources.

## Image Research Rules

Never assume internet images can be used in the app.

Best image-source process:

1. Search Google Images and Yandex Images to understand what the place looks like.
2. Search Wikimedia Commons for reusable images.
3. Check official tourism pages for reference only unless reuse permission is clear.
4. Save source links first, not copied photos.
5. For final use, prefer original YurGo photography or partner-provided images.

For each place, collect:

- Cover image
- Detail image
- Wide/context image
- Practical visitor image

## Future Research Suggestions

- Build a local verification spreadsheet for every destination.
- Ask guides in Termez and Surkhandarya to verify exact entrance pins.
- Collect original photos during field visits.
- Record seasonal differences, road conditions, and safety issues.
- Add real driver/taxi price ranges from Termez.
- Add public transport routes where possible.
- Add local restaurants, guesthouses, traditional homes, toilets, parking, and water points.
- Add Uzbek/Russian/English names for every place.
- Add "last verified" dates to every public-facing record.
